eBay AI Manager SMS Notifications

eBay AI Manager provides optional SMS notifications to its account owner for important account activity and automated action approvals.

What messages will you receive?

  • Inventory and listing notifications.
  • Sales and account activity alerts.
  • Requests to approve significant price changes.
  • Requests to approve advertising or spending changes.

How to opt in

To voluntarily subscribe, send START by text message to:

+1 (304) 801-2572

You will receive a confirmation message after successfully subscribing.

Message details

  • Message frequency varies based on account activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
  • Messages are not sent to eBay customers or other third parties.

Privacy and terms

Your phone number and messaging information are used only for the notifications described above.
