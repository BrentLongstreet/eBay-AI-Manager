Privacy Policy

Application: eBay AI Manager
Registered A2P brand: Brent Longstreet (doing business as eBay AI Manager)
Last updated: November 7, 2026

eBay AI Manager is a privately operated tool used by its sole proprietor to manage resale inventory, prepare and manage eBay listings, review marketing activity, and receive operational alerts.

Information collected

The application may store inventory and listing information, eBay authorization data needed to provide the features you enable, application settings, and the operator's mobile phone number and SMS consent record when the operator voluntarily enrolls in SMS notifications.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the inventory and listing-management features, display business activity, and—only after the operator has opted in and the messaging campaign has been approved—to send requested operational alerts and approval requests.

SMS privacy

SMS consent and the mobile number collected for SMS notifications are used only to provide the requested eBay AI Manager notifications. Mobile information and SMS opt-in data are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be processed by messaging providers solely to deliver and support the requested messages.

Storage and security

The application is currently operated locally on the operator's computer. Information is stored in the local application database and configuration files, except where a connected service is required to provide a feature. The operator is responsible for securing the computer and any connected service credentials.

Choices and retention

The operator may revoke SMS consent in the Smart Marketing dashboard. After SMS messaging is enabled, the operator may also reply STOP to an SMS to opt out. Consent records may be retained as needed to document enrollment and opt-out status.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator using the contact email or support method associated with the registered brand: twiliopolicy@peakviewmail.com.
